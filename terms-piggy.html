Puc Puc Piggy terms

Last updated: 28 October 2026. These terms cover the Puc Puc Piggy app for Android and its Premium subscription. How the app treats information is in the Puc Puc Piggy privacy notice. The website terms cover emmaplayworld.com.

The short version

  • The app is free to use with one dream. Premium unlocks more, such as more dreams and extra games.
  • The money in the app is a record of savings only. It is not a bank or a payment service and it never holds, moves or stores real money.
  • Premium is one yearly subscription, sold and billed by Google Play: a free 3-day trial for eligible new subscribers, then £2.99 a year in the UK (the local price is shown in Google Play elsewhere).
  • It renews automatically every year unless you cancel it in Google Play. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.
  • Purchases are behind a grown-ups PIN. The app is meant to be set up by a parent or carer.

Who we are

Puc Puc Piggy is made by Emma Play World. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

Using the app

Puc Puc Piggy helps a child keep a record of the money they save, with a parent or carer. The amounts are typed in by you; the app does not hold, move or store real money, is not a bank account, and is not a payment or financial service. Everything you enter stays on the device; see the privacy notice. You may use the app for your family’s own, non-commercial use. Please do not copy, sell or modify it.

What Premium includes

More dreams and extra games, as shown on the Premium screen in the app before you subscribe. The free app keeps one dream.

Premium subscription

  • One plan only: a yearly subscription, sold and billed by Google Play. There are no monthly, weekly or one-off purchases.
  • Price: £2.99 a year in the United Kingdom. In other countries Google Play shows the price in your local currency, including any tax, on the Premium screen and in Google Play before you confirm. That is the price you pay.
  • Free trial: new subscribers who are eligible get the first 3 days free. Google Play decides who is eligible (usually only Google accounts that have not had this subscription before) and shows whether a trial is offered before you confirm.
  • When you are charged: if you don’t cancel before the 3-day trial ends, Google Play charges the yearly price when the trial ends, and then again every year on the same date until you cancel. The subscription renews automatically.
  • Cancelling during the trial: cancel at any time before the trial ends and you will not be charged. Google Play normally keeps Premium on until the trial ends.
  • Cancelling after you have paid: Premium stays on until the end of the year you have already paid for, and is not renewed after that.
  • How to cancel or manage it: open Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Puc Puc Piggy, or follow the Google Play subscriptions link in the app’s grown-ups area.
  • Refunds: payments are handled by Google Play under its own terms and refund policies. Google generally does not refund the current billing period after you cancel; in some countries you may be able to cancel straight away for a partial refund. Nothing here limits any rights you have by law.
  • Price changes: if the price ever changes, Google Play tells existing subscribers in advance and, where required, asks you to agree.
  • Restoring: on a new device, or after reinstalling, signed in to the same Google account, use “Restore purchases” on the Premium screen.

Using Premium offline

Premium keeps working without an internet connection, for example in aeroplane mode. The app keeps Google Play's last confirmation of the subscription on the device and needs Google Play to confirm it again at least every 14 days, or at least once a day after the subscription has been cancelled (because its end may be close). If it can't, Premium features lock until the device is back online and Google Play confirms the subscription. Being offline never unlocks Premium on its own.

When Premium ends

When the subscription ends (at the end of a cancelled trial or paid year), or Google Play puts it on hold after a failed payment, Premium features stop until Premium is on again. Nothing is deleted: savings, the coin diary, dreams, jobs and stickers stay on the device. Because everything is saved only on the device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the records for good.

Children and purchases

The app is for children to use, but purchases, settings and links that leave the app are behind a grown-ups PIN. The app does not ask children to buy anything. If a purchase was made by mistake, contact Google Play about a refund.
