My Money privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. This notice covers the My Money app for Android (version 1.1 and later), including the free version and the optional one-time My Money Pro purchase.

The short version

  • Your financial records stay on your phone. My Money has no account, no sign-in, no server and no bank connection, and we never receive your records.
  • The free version shows one small advertising banner on the Overview screen, provided by Google AdMob. In the UK and EEA you are asked for consent first.
  • My Money Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. It removes all advertising. Google handles the payment; we never see card details.

Who we are

My Money is an earnings, payments and wallet tracker made by Emma Play World. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

Information saved on your device

Everything you enter is saved only on your phone, in the app’s own private storage:

  • jobs, hourly rates and deduction percentages;
  • pay periods, shifts, holiday, sick and off days, extra money and pay adjustments;
  • payments and their monthly paid status;
  • Wallet transactions;
  • settings such as appearance, currency and the optional app lock (the PIN itself is never stored, only a protected check value).

We do not receive, copy or back up any of this. Backups are files you choose to create and keep yourself, and they never contain your PIN.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows one banner advertisement on the Overview screen only, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, information about ad interactions, approximate location from your IP address, and diagnostic data. This is handled under Google’s advertising policies.

In the UK and EEA, Google’s consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. My Money never sends your financial records to the ad network. With My Money Pro, no ads are requested at all.

Purchases

My Money Pro is bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and tells the app whether you own Pro; we never receive your payment details.

Security

You can turn on an optional app lock with a PIN, and fingerprint or face unlock using Android’s own security. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app without the lock can see what is saved in it.

Children

My Money is a personal finance tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling My Money, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all its data from your phone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply. Please do not send financial details.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date at the top.
