Habit Dial privacy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. This notice covers the Habit Dial app for Android (version 1.0 and later), including the free version and the optional one-time lifetime unlock.

The short version

  • Your habits stay on your phone. Habit Dial has no account, no sign-in, no server and no cloud sync, and we never receive your habits or check-ins.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see card details.

Who we are

Habit Dial is a daily habit tracker made by Emma Play World. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

Information saved on your device

Everything you enter is saved only on your phone, in the app’s own private storage, encrypted with a key kept in your phone’s Android Keystore:

  • the habits you set up for each month (names, colours and order) and the days you mark as done;
  • settings such as light or dark appearance;
  • when your free trial started, and whether Google Play has confirmed a lifetime purchase;
  • the optional App Lock (the PIN itself is never stored, only a protected check value) and whether fingerprint or face unlock is on.

We do not receive, copy or back up any of this. Android’s automatic cloud backup is turned off for Habit Dial, so your habits are not copied to Google Drive or another phone. Backups are files you choose to create and keep yourself, and they never contain your PIN or purchase details.

Purchases

After a free three-day trial with all eight habits, one habit stays free forever. The lifetime unlock for all eight habits is a one-time purchase through Google Play, with no subscription. Google Play handles the payment and your Google account, shows you the price, and tells the app whether you own the unlock. The app keeps that confirmation on your phone. We never receive your payment details, and the app never sends your habits or check-ins to Google Play. Google’s handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Internet use

Habit Dial does not send your information over the internet. The only outside service it uses is Google Play, through the Google Play app on your phone, to buy, restore and check the lifetime unlock.

Security

You can turn on an optional App Lock with a 4- or 6-digit PIN, and fingerprint or face unlock using Android’s own biometric system. Your fingerprint and face data stay with Android; Habit Dial only learns whether the check succeeded. If you forget your PIN and can’t unlock with biometrics, the only way back in is to delete the app’s data on your phone.

Children

Habit Dial is a personal habit tracker for adults and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

In Habit Dial, Settings → Delete all data removes every habit and check-in and turns off App Lock. Uninstalling Habit Dial, or clearing its storage in Android settings, also deletes all its data from your phone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date at the top.
