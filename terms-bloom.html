Puc Puc Bloom terms

Last updated: 28 October 2026. These terms cover the Puc Puc Bloom app for Android (version 1.1 and later), its 3 free days and its optional one-time unlock. How the app treats information is in the Puc Puc Bloom privacy notice. The website terms cover emmaplayworld.com.

The short version

  • Everything is free for the first 3 days. No payment details are needed.
  • After 3 days, nothing is bought or charged automatically. All habits, ticks and history stay safe and can still be viewed and printed.
  • To keep adding, ticking and changing habits, a grown-up can unlock the app forever with one payment through Google Play (£2.99 in the UK; the local price is shown before you pay). There is no subscription.
  • The unlock screen is behind a grown-ups PIN.

Who we are

Puc Puc Bloom is made by Emma Play World. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

Using the app

Puc Puc Bloom is a habit tracker for one child, set up by a grown-up. Everything the app saves stays on the device; see the privacy notice. You may use the app for your family’s own, non-commercial use. Please do not copy, sell or modify it.

The 3 free days

  • For 72 hours from the first time this version of the app is opened, everything is free: up to 20 habits, ticking, the garden, the Star Jar, stickers, progress and printing.
  • This is counted by the app on the device. It is not a Google Play trial: no payment method is needed, nothing is signed up for, and nothing is charged when the 72 hours end.
  • The count uses real elapsed time, so time zones and summer time make no difference. Moving the device clock does not add free time.
  • Because there are no accounts and we do not track devices, the count is kept only on the device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes it (and all saved habits), and a new install starts a new 3 days.

Unlock forever

  • What happens after the free days without the unlock: every habit, tick, setting and the PIN are kept. Progress, history and the week chart (to print or save as a PDF) stay available, and so do the grown-ups area and Delete all. Adding, ticking and changing habits wait for the unlock. While the app is locked, habits are paused, so those days never count as missed.
  • What the unlock includes: the whole app, for good: up to 20 habits, ticking, editing, the Star Jar, stickers and the garden. There are no adverts, with or without the unlock.
  • One payment: a one-time purchase through Google Play, for the Google account that buys it. There is no subscription, no renewal and no further charge.
  • Price: £2.99 in the United Kingdom. In other countries Google Play shows the price in your local currency, including any tax, on the unlock screen and in Google Play before you confirm. That is the price you pay.
  • Pending payments: if Google Play shows a payment as pending (for example cash at a shop), the app unlocks when Google Play confirms it.
  • Restoring: after reinstalling, or on another device signed in to the same Google account, use “Restore purchases” on the unlock screen. Saved habits are not restored by this, because they are kept only on the device (Android backup may restore them if it is turned on).
  • Without internet: once Google Play has confirmed the purchase, it keeps working offline for up to 30 days at a time; after that, the app asks Google Play again when the device is online. Nothing is deleted while it waits.
  • Refunds: payments are handled by Google Play under its own terms and refund policies. If Google Play refunds or cancels a purchase, the app locks again; habits, ticks and history stay. Nothing here limits any rights you have by law.

Children and purchases

The app is for children to use, but the unlock screen, settings and links that leave the app are behind a grown-ups PIN, and “Unlock forever” asks for it again. When the free days have ended, a child who taps a habit sees a short note to ask a grown-up, with no price. The app does not ask children to buy anything. If a purchase was made by mistake, contact Google Play about a refund.
