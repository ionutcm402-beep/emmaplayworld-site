Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 October 2026. This notice covers the website emmaplayworld.com and the Puc Puc Bubbles app for Android (jump to the app section).

Who we are

This website is run by Emma Play World, the home of the game Puc Puc Bubbles. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

What this website does not do

The code of this website does not set cookies, does not use local storage, does not run analytics or advertising scripts, and has no accounts, sign-up forms, comment boxes or contact forms. We do not ask visitors for personal information on the site.

What does happen when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives technical information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is handled under Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The page loads its headline font (Baloo 2) from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore contacts Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.
  • YouTube. The site links to our YouTube channel and shows our game trailer. The trailer loads from YouTube (using youtube-nocookie.com) only after you tap it, and YouTube then handles your data under Google's privacy policy. Following a link to YouTube takes you to a service we do not control.

If you email us

If you write to contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply to you. Email is handled through Gmail (Google). We keep messages for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry. Please do not send sensitive information.

Children

The website has no accounts or forms for children. If you are a child, please ask a parent or guardian before emailing us. If you think a child has sent us personal information and you want it removed, email us and we will delete it.

The Puc Puc Bubbles app

This section covers the Puc Puc Bubbles app for Android. The app is made for young children to play with a parent or carer nearby.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts, sign-up or log-in.
  • It shows no advertising and does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It has no chat, sharing or contact with other players.
  • It does not ask for a child's name, age, photos, location or contacts, and it does not use the camera or microphone.
  • It does not send game progress or settings to us or to anyone else. We do not run any servers for the app.

What is stored on the device

To remember progress and settings, the app saves a small amount of information on the device itself: game progress (such as finished boards, stickers, treasure pals, badges and scores), game settings (such as sound volume, bubble style, colours and the chosen buddy), grown-ups settings (such as play-time limits), whether Premium is active on the device, and the grown-ups PIN, saved as a code worked out from the PIN rather than as the four digits themselves. We do not receive this information and cannot see it. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in the device's settings.

If backup is turned on for the device's Google account, Android may include the app's saved information in that backup so it can be restored on a new device. This is managed by Google under your device's backup settings, and we cannot see it.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play about Premium. The game itself works without a connection.

Premium and payments

Premium is optional and can be bought only by a grown-up, after entering the grown-ups PIN. It is sold and billed by Google Play using the Google Play billing system. Payment details are handled entirely by Google, and we never receive them. On the device, the app asks Google Play whether Premium has been bought so that it can unlock it, and it does not send this to us. Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Links that leave the app

Links to our email address, this website, our YouTube channel, the app's Google Play page and this privacy notice appear only in the grown-ups area, behind the PIN. Opening one leaves the app, and the service you open handles your data under its own policy.

Children and the app

The app is designed for children and does not collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data held about you, such as asking to see it, correct it or delete it, or to object to how it is used, and a right to complain to your local data protection authority. To ask us about data we hold, email us.

Changes

If we change how the website or the app works in a way that affects this notice, we will update this page and its date. For the app, we do this before the new version is released.
