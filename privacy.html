Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026. This notice covers the website emmaplayworld.com only.

Who we are

This website is run by Emma Play World, the home of the game Puc Puc Bubbles. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

What this website does not do

The code of this website does not set cookies, does not use local storage, does not run analytics or advertising scripts, and has no accounts, sign-up forms, comment boxes or contact forms. We do not ask visitors for personal information on the site.

What does happen when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives technical information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is handled under Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The page loads its headline font (Baloo 2) from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore contacts Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.
  • YouTube. The site links to our YouTube channel. When videos are added to the site, a video loads from YouTube (using youtube-nocookie.com) only after you tap it, and YouTube then handles your data under Google's privacy policy. Following a link to YouTube takes you to a service we do not control.

If you email us

If you write to contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply to you. Email is handled through Gmail (Google). We keep messages for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry. Please do not send sensitive information.

Children

The website has no accounts or forms for children. If you are a child, please ask a parent or guardian before emailing us. If you think a child has sent us personal information and you want it removed, email us and we will delete it.

The game

This notice does not describe how the Puc Puc Bubbles app handles data. Information about the app belongs in its own privacy information and store listing.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data held about you, such as asking to see it, correct it or delete it, or to object to how it is used, and a right to complain to your local data protection authority. To ask us about data we hold, email us.

Changes

If we change how the website works in a way that affects this notice, we will update this page and its date.
