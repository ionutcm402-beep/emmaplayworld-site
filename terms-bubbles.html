Puc Puc Bubbles terms

Last updated: 28 October 2026. These terms cover the Puc Puc Bubbles app for Android and its one-time Premium purchase. How the app treats information is in the Puc Puc Bubbles privacy notice. The website terms cover emmaplayworld.com.

The short version

  • When the game is first installed, everything is open free for 3 days (72 hours). No payment details are needed and nothing is bought automatically.
  • After the 3 days, three boards stay free: Letters, Pet friends and Shape bubbles. A grown-up can unlock everything forever with one optional purchase.
  • Premium is a one-time purchase, sold and billed by Google Play: £2.99 in the UK (the local price is shown in Google Play elsewhere). It is not a subscription and never renews.
  • Purchases are behind a grown-ups PIN. The app is meant to be set up by a parent or carer.

Who we are

Puc Puc Bubbles is made by Emma Play World. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

Using the app

Puc Puc Bubbles is a bubble-popping learning game for young children, played with a parent or carer nearby. Everything the game saves stays on the device; see the privacy notice. You may use the app for your family’s own, non-commercial use. Please do not copy, sell or modify it.

What Premium includes

All 152 boards (numbers, animals, fruits, colours, objects and more) and the grown-ups daily play-time and board limits, with extra time or boards when you choose. A grown-up reaches the Premium screen after the grown-ups PIN.

The 3 free days

  • What it is: the game itself opens everything for the first 72 hours after it is first started on a device. It is not a Google Play trial: no Google account details, card or payment are asked for, and nothing is charged or bought when it ends.
  • When it ends: after 72 hours the Premium boards lock and the three free boards stay open. The game shows a short message once. Nothing is deleted.
  • How it is counted: on the device itself, without the internet. Moving the device clock backwards does not add more time. Because the game keeps no account and sends nothing to us, the free days are counted per installation; they are a goodwill preview, not a promise.

Premium purchase

  • One purchase only: “Unlock forever” is a single, optional, one-time purchase, sold and billed by Google Play. There are no subscriptions, renewals or recurring charges, and nothing else to buy.
  • Price: £2.99 in the United Kingdom. In other countries Google Play shows the price in your local currency, including any tax, on the Premium screen and in Google Play before you confirm. That is the price you pay.
  • What “forever” means: Premium stays unlocked for as long as the app is available on Google Play for your Google account, including future updates and boards we add, with no time limit and without needing the internet once it has been confirmed.
  • Pending payments: if Google Play says a payment is pending (for example cash or bank payments), Premium unlocks when Google Play confirms the payment.
  • Restoring: on a new device, or after reinstalling, signed in to the same Google account, use “Restore purchases” on the Premium screen.
  • Refunds: payments are handled by Google Play under its own terms and refund policies. If a purchase is refunded or cancelled by Google Play, Premium locks again. Nothing here limits any rights you have by law.
  • Earlier test versions: closed testers of earlier versions who used a test subscription do not keep Premium from it; the new purchase is separate.

If Premium is locked

Nothing is deleted. Stars, finished boards, the collection and settings stay on the device. The Premium boards lock and the three free boards stay open. (Game progress is saved only on the device, so uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes it.)

Children and purchases

The app is for children to use, but purchases, settings and links that leave the app are behind a grown-ups PIN. The app does not ask children to buy anything. If a purchase was made by mistake, contact Google Play about a refund.
