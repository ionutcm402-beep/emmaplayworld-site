Puc Puc Pegs privacy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. This notice covers the Puc Puc Pegs game for Android, and the terms of its optional Premium subscription. The full Puc Puc Pegs terms are on their own page.

The short version

  • The game collects no personal information from children or grown-ups: no accounts, no sign-in and no location.
  • It has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party tools that collect data.
  • Stars, stickers, settings and the grown-ups PIN stay on the device. We never receive them.
  • Premium is bought through Google Play, which handles the payment. We never see card details.
  • Premium, settings and links that leave the game are behind a grown-ups PIN.

Who we are

Puc Puc Pegs is a peg-board picture game for young children, made by Emma Play World. This notice explains how the game treats information, and the terms of the optional Premium subscription. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, store on our servers or share any personal information. That includes names, ages, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, advertising IDs and device identifiers.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

The friendly voice that reads messages aloud uses the device’s own built-in text-to-speech. The game sends it only short game phrases such as “Find a yellow ring”, never any personal information, and the voice can be switched off in the grown-ups area.

Information saved on the device

To remember progress, the game saves a few things on the device only:

  • which pictures are finished, stars and stickers;
  • game settings (voice, hints, sound, break reminder and similar);
  • the 4-digit grown-ups PIN you choose;
  • whether Premium is on, and when Google Play last confirmed it (so Premium keeps working without internet for up to 14 days).

This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us. You can clear progress in the grown-ups area (“Reset progress”) or by clearing the app’s storage in the device’s settings, and everything is removed when the game is uninstalled.

Android backup is turned off for the game, so its saved information is not copied to Google Drive or to a new device. Because of this, uninstalling the game deletes stars, stickers and progress for good; a Premium subscription is not lost and can be restored from Google Play.

The game asks Android for only two permissions: internet access (used only to talk to Google Play about Premium) and Google Play billing.

Children’s privacy

Puc Puc Pegs is designed for children and was designed with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the EU and UK GDPR, and the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in mind. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side.

Settings and purchases are behind a grown-ups PIN, so children cannot change them or buy anything on their own. Links that leave the game (this notice, email and Manage subscription) appear only in the grown-ups area, behind the PIN. The service you open handles your data under its own policy.

Payments and Google Play

Premium is sold as a subscription through Google Play, using the Google Play billing system. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and we never receive card or payment details.

On the device, the game asks Google Play whether this Google account has an active Premium subscription, so that it can unlock the pictures. It does not send this to us. Google may show us basic order information in our Google Play developer account, such as an order number, date, country and amount, which we use only for accounting, refunds and support.

App performance reports from Google

We do not add any crash-reporting or analytics tools to the game. Like all apps on Google Play, Google may give us general reports about crashes and freezes (“Android vitals”). These are collected by Android from devices whose owners have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google, not by the game, and they are handled under Google’s privacy policy. They do not identify you or your child to us, and we use them only to fix problems.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Subscription terms

  • What’s free: 2 pictures in each level (Easy, Medium and Tricky), with no time limit.
  • What Premium adds: all 60 pictures and all stickers.
  • One plan only: a yearly subscription that renews automatically every year unless you cancel it. There are no monthly, weekly or one-off purchases.
  • Free trial: eligible new subscribers get the first 3 days free. Google Play decides who is eligible (usually only Google accounts that have not had this subscription before) and shows whether a trial is offered before you confirm. Cancel at any time before the trial ends and you will not be charged; Premium stays on until the trial ends.
  • Price and renewal: £2.99 a year in the United Kingdom; in other countries Google Play shows the local price, including any tax, before you confirm. If you don’t cancel before the trial ends, Google Play charges the yearly price when the trial ends, then again every year on the same date. It renews automatically until you cancel.
  • Cancelling: cancel any time in Google Play under Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, or with “Manage subscription” in the grown-ups area. After you have paid, Premium stays on until the end of the year already paid for.
  • When Premium ends: stars, stickers and finished pictures stay on the device; only the Premium pictures lock again.
  • Refunds: refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. Google generally does not refund the current billing period after you cancel; in some countries you may be able to cancel straight away for a partial refund. Nothing here limits your rights under consumer law.
  • Without internet: once Google Play has confirmed Premium, it keeps working offline for up to 14 days after the last confirmation, then waits for the next check (automatic when online). Being offline never turns Premium on by itself.
  • Restoring: if you reinstall the game or use a new device with the same Google account, tap “Restore purchases” on the Premium screen in the grown-ups area.

Keeping information safe

Everything the game saves stays in its own private storage on the device, which Android keeps separate from other apps. We run no servers for the game, so there is no copy on our side to protect or to lose. The game talks to Google Play over Google’s encrypted connection, and payment details never pass through the game or through us. Anyone who can unlock the device and open the game can see what is saved in it, so keep the device locked and use the grown-ups PIN for grown-up areas.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply. Please do not send sensitive information.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change ever meant collecting any information, we would ask for a parent’s permission first.
