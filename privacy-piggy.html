Puc Puc Piggy privacy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. This notice covers the Puc Puc Piggy app for Android (version 1.0.21 and later). See also the app terms.

WebsitePuc Puc BubblesPuc Puc BloomPuc Puc PiggyPuc Puc Pegs

The short version

  • The app collects no personal information: no accounts, no sign-up and no location. A first name for the little saver is optional and stays on the device.
  • It has no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • The piggy's money, dreams, stickers and settings stay on the device. We never receive them, and Android backup is turned off for the app.
  • Everything is free for 3 days. After that, the optional one-time unlock is sold by Google Play, and we never see payment details.
  • Buying, settings and links that leave the app are behind a grown-ups PIN.

Who we are

Puc Puc Piggy is a piggy bank app for children, made by Emma Play World. It is designed for a child to use with a parent or carer. The money in the app is a record of savings only: it is not real money, the app does not hold or move any, and it is not a bank, a payment service or any other financial service. You can contact us at contact.emmaplayworld@gmail.com.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts, sign-up or log-in.
  • It does not ask for a child's age, photos, location or contacts, and it does not use the camera or microphone. A first name or nickname can be typed in Settings if you wish; it is optional and stays on the device.
  • It shows no advertising and does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It has no chat, sharing or contact with other people.
  • It does not send amounts, dreams or settings to us or to anyone else. We do not run any servers for the app.

What is stored on the device

To work, the app saves a small amount of information on the device itself:

  • the amounts added to or taken out of the piggy, where each came from, and the coin diary;
  • the dreams you create (a picture, a name you type and a price) and your progress towards them;
  • jobs to earn, stickers, scores in the games and any pocket money settings;
  • the optional first name or nickname of the little saver;
  • settings (money type, sounds, moving pictures and whether taking money out needs the PIN);
  • the grown-ups PIN;
  • when the free 3 days started on this device, and the latest time the app has seen (so moving the clock back can't add free time);
  • whether Google Play has confirmed the one-time unlock, and when it last did (so the unlock keeps working offline for up to 30 days);
  • whether the private app review code has been entered on the device (it is given only to Google Play reviewers and unlocks the app without a payment).

We do not receive this information and cannot see it. Please do not type personal details, such as a child's full name, into a dream's name. Even if you do, it stays on the device.

Grown-ups › Delete all in the app clears the money, the coin diary, dreams, jobs and stickers; the settings (including the PIN and money type) and the unlock stay. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in the device's settings, or uninstall the app.

Android backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the app, so its saved information is not copied to Google Drive or to a new device. Uninstalling the app deletes it for good.

Sounds and vibration

The app plays its own built-in sounds and may make the device vibrate. It does not record anything. Sounds can be turned off in Settings.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play about the one-time unlock (its price and whether it was bought). Everything else, including its pictures and fonts, is built into the app and works without a connection.

The free 3 days and the one-time unlock

Everything in the app is free for 3 days from the first time this version is opened. No payment details are needed and nothing is bought automatically. The free days are counted on the device only: we use no accounts and no device identifiers to track them, so they are not linked to you or to the device in any way we can see.

After 3 days, a grown-up can unlock everything forever with one purchase through Google Play, using the Google Play billing system (£2.99 in the UK, or the local price Google Play shows). It is not a subscription and nothing renews. Without it, everything already saved can still be seen. The full terms are in the Puc Puc Piggy terms.

Payment details are handled entirely by Google, and we never receive them. On the device, the app asks Google Play for the price and whether the unlock has been bought, and it does not send this to us. Google may show us basic order information, such as an order number, date, country and amount, which we use only for accounting, refunds and support. Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy. Closed-test versions up to 1.0.20 offered a yearly subscription instead; it can be cancelled in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

App performance reports from Google

We do not add any crash-reporting or analytics tools to the app. Like all apps on Google Play, Google may give us general reports about crashes and freezes (“Android vitals”). These are collected by Android from devices whose owners have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google, not by the app, and they are handled under Google's privacy policy.

Links that leave the app

Links to our email address, this website, our YouTube channel, Puc Puc Bubbles and Puc Puc Bloom on this website, the app terms and this privacy notice appear only in the grown-ups area and on the Unlock Forever screen, behind the PIN. Opening one leaves the app, and the service you open handles your data under its own policy.

Keeping information safe

Everything the app saves stays in its own private storage on the device, which Android keeps separate from other apps. We run no servers for the app, so there is no copy on our side to protect or to lose. The app talks to Google Play over Google’s encrypted connection, and payment details never pass through the app or through us. Anyone who can unlock the device and open the app can see what is saved in it, so keep the device locked and use the grown-ups PIN for grown-up areas.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply. Email is handled through Gmail (Google). We keep messages for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry. Please do not send sensitive information.

Children and the app

The app is designed for children and does not collect personal information from them. There is no advertising, chat or sharing, and purchases, settings and outside links are behind the grown-ups PIN. If you think a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data held about you, such as asking to see it, correct it or delete it, or to object to how it is used, and a right to complain to your local data protection authority. The app sends us no personal data, so we hold none from your use of it. To ask about anything else, such as an email you sent us, email us.

Changes

If we change how the app works in a way that affects this notice, we will update this page and its date before the new version is released.
